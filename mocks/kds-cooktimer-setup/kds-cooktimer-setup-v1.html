What changed from the version currently on staging

  • Home button in the header (portal rule: every screen has one) — it was a back arrow only.
  • Green for every action, matching the rest of the portal — the “Change” link and search focus were sky-blue.
  • Bigger tap targets (44px) on the delete, drag and step controls — several were 32px, too small for kitchen hands.
  • Proper line icons instead of emoji for delete/drag chrome.
  • Fixed station colours (Grill orange, Fryer amber, Oven red) instead of a palette that cycled to violet/pink as stations were added.
  • Plainer words: “Dish” not “Product”, “Name shown to cooks”, “On — shows in the cooks’ TO COOK list”.
